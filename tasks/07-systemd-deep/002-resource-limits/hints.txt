MemoryMax and CPUQuota both go inside the [Service] section, one directive per line, same as ExecStart.
Editing the unit file alone isn't enough — systemd needs to reread it: sudo systemctl daemon-reload
Limits only take effect on the running unit after a restart: sudo systemctl restart labdemo
Verify what systemd actually applied (not just what's in the file) with: systemctl show labdemo -p MemoryMax -p CPUQuotaPerSecUSec
